How I tend to work

  1. 01 Understand the failure.

    Find the actual constraint before designing around it.

  2. 02 Make the cheap version first.

    Foam, plywood, printed plastic, rough machining — whatever answers the question fastest.

  3. 03 Measure it.

    A prototype that only “feels better” has not finished its job.

  4. 04 Then make it good.

    Simplify parts, tolerances, assembly and service.

About

Ashley is a mechanical engineer working across product design, prototyping and test.

I like problems that exist in the physical world: things that vibrate, wear out, get hot, don't fit, cost too much to make, or fail in ways nobody expected.

Most of my work sits somewhere between CAD and a workbench.
